# Regnum: Prompts for Gemini

Seven reference images, one per scene. Give Gemini each image with the shared instructions below, followed by that scene's description.

The game puts clickable spots on top of these pictures, so **the layout must stay the same**. Every person and object has to stay where it is in the reference. If anything drifts, open the game, press the gear (top left), and re-tap the spots for that scene.

---

## Paste this first, every time

> Repaint the attached image as a detailed, realistic historical illustration, in the style of a high-quality painted museum or textbook illustration: natural light, rich textures, believable faces and clothing, warm painterly color.
>
> **Keep the composition exactly the same.** Every person and object must stay in the same position, at the same size, facing the same way, doing the same thing. Do not add, remove, or move any figure or object. Keep the same very wide panoramic shape (about 3.2 to 1).
>
> **Setting:** the city of Rome and its countryside in the summer of **133 BC**, under the Roman Republic. Use plain stone, brick, timber, painted plaster, terracotta roof tiles, and wool and linen clothing. **Do not include:** the Colosseum, marble emperors, legionaries in armor, gladiators, "SPQR," medieval or modern items, or glass windows.
>
> **People:** Mediterranean Romans with a realistic range of skin tones, plus Greeks, Syrians, Spaniards, and Italians where described. Men are mostly clean-shaven with short hair, except where a beard is described. Citizens wear white wool togas over tunics; senators' togas have a purple-red stripe. Working people wear belted knee-length tunics in brown, grey, and faded colors. Women wear long stolas with a mantle (palla) that can cover the head. Faces should be expressive and dignified, never cartoonish. The audience is students aged 12 to 15, so nothing gory.
>
> Do not add any writing, except the short inscriptions listed in the scene description.

## 1_nasica_atrium.jpg: House of Scipio Nasica (atrium)

A wealthy senator's atrium in Rome, bright midday. Sunlight falls through a square opening in the timber-beamed roof onto a shallow marble rain pool in the floor. The walls are painted plaster made to look like rows of ochre marble blocks above a deep red dado. The floor is red mortar dotted with white stone chips.

Left to right:
- A small painted household shrine on the wall, showing two dancing youths with drinking horns and a snake.
- A polished bronze hand mirror on a stand.
- Four dark wooden cupboards holding pale wax ancestor masks, with the words CORNELII SCIPIONES above them.
- A dark study behind red curtains, with shelves of scrolls. In front of it stands a tall grey-haired senator in a toga with a purple stripe, gesturing (Scipio Nasica).
- A long wooden table holding wax tablets, one tied with red cord and sealed, and a silver jug.
- Two plain columns, with the rain pool between them reflecting the sky and a few floating petals.
- A balding senator in a striped toga, holding a silver wine cup.
- An old grey-haired slave woman in a brown dress and mantle, folding linen: the children's nurse.
- An older bearded Greek steward in a blue tunic, holding tablets.
- A tall upright weaving loom with red and cream threads and clay loom weights hanging at the bottom.
- A small table with a silver dish of purple figs.
- A leather satchel on the floor.
- An old doorkeeper slave on a stool beside the bright open street door.

## 2_forum_day.jpg: The Forum Romanum

The Forum on a sunny midsummer day, paved with pale stone and busy with people, including many women.

Left to right:
- The Senate house: a tall brick building with a pediment and big bronze doors, and the word CVRIA.
- A columned portico with charcoal graffiti on its plaster wall: TI GRACCHE REDDE AGRVM PVBLICVM PAVPERIBVS, and smaller, WE FOVGHT FOR IT.
- In front: a Vestal Virgin, a woman in a long white robe with a white veil and woollen headbands, walking calmly. Beside her is an attendant in red carrying a bundle of rods (fasces).
- The speakers' platform, decorated with bronze ship-rams. On it, a young man in a white toga speaks with one arm raised (Tiberius Gracchus).
- A sundial on a short stone pillar, beside a wooden bench where a senator sits.
- A serious senator standing a little apart.
- Behind everything, the green Palatine hill, covered with tiled houses and cypresses.
- Three sunburned country men in brown tunics, one leaning on a staff.
- A fenced patch of old paving in the foreground, with a few coins in it.
- The Temple of Castor, with Doric columns.
- A Greek money-changer at a wooden table with scales and stacks of coins.
- Three attendants in red tunics, each carrying fasces (bundles of rods tied with red straps).
- The Ionic Temple of Saturn.
- Wooden bleachers under construction.
- A red-faced man shouting from on top of a barrel.
- A girl selling roasted chickpeas from a brazier.
- A stone city gate arch at the far right.

## 3_subura_insula.jpg: The Subura (tenement courtyard)

A crowded courtyard surrounded by a five-storey brick-and-timber apartment block (insula). It has shuttered windows, wooden balconies with laundry hanging, and dark shop openings on the ground floor. The ground is dusty.

Left to right:
- A small crossroads shrine on the wall, hung with little wool dolls.
- A widow sitting in a doorway, spinning wool on a drop spindle (Naevia), with a twelve-year-old boy beside her.
- A shop counter with clay pots sunk into it and steam rising, and a woman serving behind it.
- A tall empty clay storage jar in the foreground.
- Three small children crouched in the dust, playing knucklebones.
- Up on the second floor, a woman at a window tipping out a clay pot.
- A grizzled, scarred veteran with a grey beard writing on wax tablets at a table, with stacked tablets on it (Quintus Arrius).
- Two country men waiting in line.
- A barefoot girl of about eleven balancing a clay water jar on her head (Tertia).
- A long crack in the wall high above, propped with diagonal timbers.
- A heavy, balding rent collector holding a tablet and a long stick.
- A sly man leaning on a wooden post.
- A fuller's shop with a man treading cloth in a vat.
- A stone public fountain with water running from a pipe into a basin.

## 4_tiburtina_estate.jpg: The estate on the Via Tiburtina

The Roman countryside on a golden afternoon, with hazy blue mountains behind and golden wheat stubble in front.

Left to right:
- An old silver-green olive tree beside an old white-bearded farmer with a hoe, standing at a crooked wooden fence with bean rows.
- A small ruined mud-brick farmhouse with a collapsed tile roof.
- Rows of grapevines on stakes, with a small weathered stone family tomb among them.
- A squat stone boundary marker in the foreground with AGER PVBL carved on it, and a scraped patch of dirt where it used to stand.
- A slave overseer with a black beard, in a good blue tunic, holding a whip.
- A line of six chained field slaves hoeing, linked at the ankles.
- A long low stone barracks with high slit windows.
- On a hill, a white villa with a long colonnade, surrounded by cypresses.
- A hare in the stubble.
- A slave woman carrying a basket of bread (Philumena).
- A young Syrian shepherd with a crook and a small flock of sheep.
- A muleteer leading a pack mule loaded with clay jars down a stone-paved road.
- A Roman milestone carved XVI.

## 5_gracchus_house.jpg: House of the Gracchi

A busy, lived-in Roman house in warm afternoon light. In the middle, a columned peristyle garden with cypresses and a small pool. The walls are painted plaster blocks over a dark green dado.

Left to right:
- The open street door, with ordinary people visible gathering outside.
- A dignified grey-haired woman in purple, seated very straight in a high-backed chair, holding a scroll (Cornelia).
- A small table with an empty ivory jewel box.
- A wall shelf of rolled letters, with the word EPISTVLAE beneath it.
- Three scribes seated at a long table copying wax tablets, with baskets of tablets on the floor.
- A large linen survey map gridded into squares, with one block outlined in red.
- A young man of twenty-one in a white tunic with a surveyor's groma: a pole with a cross on top and hanging plumb weights (Gaius Gracchus).
- A woman in blue sitting in the garden doorway, very still (Sempronia).
- A bearded Greek philosopher with a scroll.
- A reading stand holding an open Greek scroll.
- A Greek speechwriter in green with a wax tablet.
- A golden crown shaped like city walls with battlements, in an arched wall niche.
- A child's wax writing tablet and a stylus on the floor.
- A cedar chest bound in bronze, with a starburst emblem.
- A young woman in a rose-colored stola (Claudia), with a small boy in a white toga beside her.

## 6_vigil_night.jpg: The night before the vote

A Roman street at night under a starry sky and a crescent moon, lit by firelight and torches.

Left to right:
- Country men asleep in their cloaks beside a small fire, with one man sitting up awake.
- A dark, barred doorway in the apartment block above.
- A grizzled veteran with a grey beard, holding tablets.
- A girl with a clay water jar on her head, among a few people standing in the street (Tertia).
- In the center, the lit doorway of a great house with torches on both sides. A young man in a dark mourning toga stands in it (Tiberius Gracchus), and a small boy in a white toga stands just below him.
- An old soldier's helmet with a red crest on the doorstep.
- A stocky bearded man with one hand raised to his head (Gaius Villius).
- A big loud man raising a cup.
- A second small fire.
- A widow on a doorstep, with a sleeping boy across her lap (Naevia).
- Far right, high on a hill: the pale Temple of Jupiter on the Capitol in the moonlight, with a smaller lit temple below it.

## 7_fides_night.jpg: The Temple of Fides, before dawn

Inside a stone temple porch with Ionic columns, lit by bronze oil lamps. At the right, the temple opens onto a grey-pink dawn sky over the Capitol, with a temple and a gathering crowd.

Left to right:
- An old, balding consul in a striped toga, seated alone beside a tall bronze lamp (Mucius Scaevola).
- A wooden bench with a wax tablet on it.
- A pile of rough wooden staves and sawn-off bench legs.
- A long table holding a sealed tablet with a red seal.
- A marble statue of the goddess Fides (Good Faith) on a pedestal, her right hand held out open. FIDES is carved on the pedestal.
- On the table: a red account book, a folded toga, a bronze ladle, and a sacrificial knife with an ivory handle.
- A tribune in a white toga holding a bench leg like a club.
- A slave carrying more staves.
- A Roman matron in a dark stola and veil, holding a small offering, standing against the dawn (Fannia).
- A small pen of white geese.
- Dawn breaking over the Temple of Jupiter, with a crowd assembling below.
